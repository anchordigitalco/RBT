Our partners

Teaching is complex, learnable professional work.

Founded in 1979, Research for Better Teaching provides research-based professional development for K-12 educators, grounded in research, analysis of practice, and a deep respect for the intellectual demands of teaching.

Our flagship programs, Studying Skillful Teaching and Analyzing Teaching for Student Results, draw on the proven frameworks of The Skillful Teacher to deliver research-based, practical strategies that improve student learning in every classroom.
